# Sample attribution

The nine demo images and six MusicXML ground-truth files are derived from the
**OLiMPiC 1.0 – Scanned** dataset, which is distributed under
**CC BY-SA 4.0**. The dataset is available at
<https://hdl.handle.net/11234/1-5419>.

- Real scans and their MusicXML transcriptions: OLiMPiC sample IDs 4919798 and
  4976604, and 6548435.
- "Clean" images were rendered from the OLiMPiC MusicXML with Verovio by the
  original research prototype.
- "Phone photo", "hard low-res" and other degraded variants were synthesised
  from those materials by the original research prototype's scripts.

The full CC BY-SA 4.0 text is in `LICENSE-OLiMPiC.txt`, and the dataset README
is in `README-OLiMPiC.md`. If you redistribute this repository or a deployed
service using these samples, keep this attribution and the licence with it.

These files are demo/evaluation data only; they are not used to train or tune
anything, and all outputs are produced at request time by the OMR engines.
